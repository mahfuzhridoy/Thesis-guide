# Questions
Before selceting a thesis topic, there are few questions a researcher should ask.

## Interest and motivation
1. Am i genuinely interested in this topic?
2. Can i stay motivated for several months to work on this thesis?
3. Does it align with my long term career goal.

## Problem Identification
1. What specific problem am i trying to solve?
2. Why is this problem important?
3. Who benefits if this problem is solved?
4. What are the consequences if the problem remains unsolved?

## Research Gap
1. What has already been done
2. What is still missing in the existing literature?
3. Can i clearly identify a research gap?
4. Is my work improving existing methods or proposing something new?

## Originality
1. Is this topic novel enough for my degree?
2. Has someone already solved this exact problem?
3. If similar work exists, what makes my approach different?

## Research questions
1. Can i express my study as one clear research?
2. Can it be divided into logical sub questions?
3. Are these questions answerable through research?

## Objectives
1. What are my primary objectives?
2. Are they specific and measureable?
3. Will acheiving these objectives answer my research questions?

## Feasibility
1. Can i complete this within the available time?
2. Do I hev the required skill?
3. Is the required hardware or software available?

## Data availability
1. What data do i need?
2. Can I legally obtain it?
3. Is the data sufficient?
4. How will i collect it?

## Methodology
1. Which research method is appropriate?
    - Experimental
    - Survey
    - Case study
    - Simulation
    - Quantitavive
    - Qualitative
2. Can i justify my methodology

## Resources
1. What software or tool Required?
2. Do i need special equipment?
3. Will the research require funding?

## Evaluation
1. How will i easure success?
2. What metrics will i use?
3. How will i compare my solution against existing approach?

## Limitation
1. What are the expected limitations?
2. What assumptions am i making?
3. What is outside the scope of my research?

## Ethical and legal Issues
1. Does this research involve human participants?
2. Are the privacy or security concerns?
3. Do i need ethical approval?

## Supervisor Support
1. Does my supervisor have expertise in this area?
2. Can they provide meaningful guidance?
3. Are they interested in supervising this topic?

## Publication Potential
1. Could this work become a conference or journal paper?
2. Would it strengthen my portfolio or CV?

## Would it strengthen my portfolio or CV?
1. Can this research be applied in industry?
2. Will organizations or researchers find it useful?
3. Does it solve a real-world problem?


*Note: To do a strong thesis, It is not required to have the all answer "yes" but, most of them.*
